### DeepSeek-pro

Think of JEV as the door policy for a club that only lets in proven regulars: no velvet rope theatrics, just a bouncer who checks your track record before you touch the dance floor. Speculators—those who arrive with vibes, borrowed conviction, or a single lucky night—are turned away at the threshold. To earn entry, a claim must show a hit-rate of at least 70% across no fewer than 20 independent sessions, a ledger of repeated performance rather than one bright flash. And JEV is honest enough to enforce the same standard on itself: if a past promotion later fails to hold that line, JEV demotes it, no nostalgia, no grandfather clause. The bouncer does not flatter the guest list; it revises it.

### Qwen3-80B

JEV is the bouncer at the velvet-rope club of truth, where only verified insights earn entry—not hype, not half-baked hypotheses, not speculators waving shiny graphs like fake VIP passes. It doesn’t care about pedigree or charisma; it demands a hard-hit rate of ≥70% across ≥20 validated sessions, each one a fingerprint on the ledger of reliability. Like a jury of skeptical art critics who’ve seen every fake masterpiece, JEV scrutinizes every embedding for latent inconsistency, every correlation for accidental alignment. It doesn’t just reject the weak—it retroactively demotes its own past promotions when new evidence exposes decay, tearing down golden tickets it once handed out with misplaced trust. No exceptions. No grace periods. No “maybe next time.” JEV doesn’t gatekeep—it *validates*, and its door only opens for those who prove, repeatedly, that they belong. In this club, the only thing louder than the bass is the silence of the failed.
