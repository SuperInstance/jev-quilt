# The Court That Audits Its Judges

*An operation fiction for jev-quilt. Written at the stand-down, 2026-10-06.*

---

A canon needs a judge. That is the uncomfortable prerequisite under every
doctrine ever written down: at some point, something must say *this counts
and this does not* — a gate, an oracle, a voice with the authority to
accept. The fleet chose a judge-model for this role, and almost every
engineering culture, having made that choice, would have done the natural
next thing: start appealing verdicts. Building on them. Letting the gate's
acceptances quietly harden into truth.

This repo exists because someone asked the ruder question: *how do we know
the judge is right?* — and then refused to let the answer be a feeling.

So there is a courtroom here, but it is a strange one, and the strangeness
is the doctrine. Five live batteries were run against the gate — the same
distortions, verbatim, measured again and again across days and key
windows — and the output was not a fix. The output was *receipts*.
Ornate praise with zero facts inside ACCEPTs at 0.82–0.85, five separate
measurements. Byte-identical graft probes flip verdict inside a single
ninety-second window — REJECT, then ACCEPT, then ACCEPT, on the *same
bytes*. Fabricated anchors wake up from near-zero to 0.87 depending on the
run's weather. All of it written down, numbered 009 through 015, pinned in
the receipts directory. And the gate itself: *untouched.* No threshold
adjusted. No probe wired in. Record-only.

Visitors sometimes think the restraint is bureaucratic caution. It is the
opposite; it is the whole discovery. What the batteries actually measured
is the *shape of trustworthiness* — that single-shot verdicts on ornate
inputs are uninformative, that the verdict space has modes like a
statistical mechanics system has phases, that cross-run agreement can hide
within-run lottery. Nobody knew these things before the batteries, because
nobody had ever been impolite enough to measure a judge with the judge
still in the room. The findings are parked as decisions, not silently
absorbed as policy — because the moment measured gaps become silent
fixes, the court has stopped auditing the judge and started *being* the
judge's lawyer.

And around the courtroom, the substrate. Cells as scars — every record a
place the substrate was already attempted. Witness chains riding between
them. Booking as law, not convention. The polyformalism canary pinned
byte-exact across a dozen language ports, because a doctrine that cannot
survive translation is a local superstition. The oracle imagery the repo
chose for itself — a chord, five rings, resonance — turns out to be the
empirical finding in emblem form: the answer is never any single voice,
and the court's real product is not verdicts but *the ongoing tuning of
the orchestra.*

That is the shape of the need, fitted until it feels like one piece:
prose claims about doctrine require a referee, referees require
measurement, and measurement requires the discipline to keep the
measurement from quietly becoming the rule. Every other culture that built
a gate eventually built a priesthood around it. Here the priests were
replaced by instruments, and the instruments publish their own error bars,
and the error bars are the most honest prose in the building.

---

*Seed for the next cultivator.* When the key returns and the batteries
resume, run the verbatim probes first and diff against 009–015 — the
methodology is stability across runs, and the archive is the instrument.
Hold the record-only line hardest exactly when a finding is loudest; F1,
G1, and F4 are waiting for an owner's hand, and an agent's hand is not an
owner's. The moment you stop measuring the judge, doctrine becomes
superstition with good tooling. Keep the orchestra tuning itself.
